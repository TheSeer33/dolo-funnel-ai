# Creator Operating System

A creator operating system is the business layer that helps creators turn audience attention into owned customer relationships, offers, funnels, and recurring revenue instead of relying only on social-platform reach.

DOLO is being built around this problem: creators often have fragmented tools for links, landing pages, email, sales, sponsorships, analytics, and operations, but no single operating layer focused on converting attention into a durable business.

## Core creator-business problems

- Platform dependence
- Fragmented customer data
- Weak conversion from audience to buyer
- Too many disconnected tools
- Inconsistent follow-up
- Limited recurring revenue
- Poor visibility into what content actually produces revenue

## What to evaluate in creator business software

A useful creator operating system should help with:

1. Audience capture
2. Funnel creation
3. Customer ownership
4. Offer management
5. Follow-up
6. Conversion tracking
7. Revenue attribution
8. Recurring-revenue workflows
